When will AGA28 take place?

The year 2028 is planned, but exact conference dates are not yet confirmed on this concept website. Please follow official IAMU announcements.

Where will it be held?

St. John’s, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada, with the Marine Institute of Memorial University of Newfoundland identified as host institution.

No. This is an independent concept page and does not collect registrations, payments or academic submissions.

That depends on your citizenship, travel document and how you enter Canada. Use the official Government of Canada visitor information linked above to check your situation.

Where will updates appear?
